# Consent for Raw Footage (Task S-02)

Everyone who appears in raw footage (teammates and friends only) signs this before any recording. A clip without a signed form is rejected by the eval manifest (`consent: true` is required, see the clip manifest schema in `docs/tasks/P4.md` P4-06).

**Signed forms are never committed.** Keep them in a private folder outside the repo. The repo holds this template and, per clip, only the `consent: true` flag in the manifest.

## Form

> **Rulecam demo recording: consent**
>
> I agree to be filmed for the Rulecam hackathon project (Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon, Oct 26 – Nov 4 2026).
>
> 1. **What is recorded:** video of me performing staged actions on a prop set (kitchen, yard, corridor or warehouse tabletop). No audio is used for identification, and ambient speech is never transcribed.
> 2. **What it is used for:** testing and evaluating the system, and excerpts in the project video and README. Raw footage may be processed on rented GPUs (standard privacy mode).
> 3. **What it is not used for:** identifying me, measuring my personal performance, or any purpose outside this project.
> 4. **Anonymous renders:** footage may be shown as a skeleton on a blank background instead of my face and body.
> 5. **Withdrawal:** I can ask for my clips to be removed from the repo, the eval set and the video until the submission deadline (Nov 4 2026, 12:00 ET). After that, clips already published cannot be recalled.
>
> Name: ______________________  Date: ____________
> Signature: __________________
> Contact (to withdraw): ______________________

## Collecting and checking

- [ ] One signed form per person per recording day (or one for the whole weekend, if the form says so).
- [ ] Keep an index (name, date, which sets) next to the forms, outside the repo.
- [ ] Before each recording session, check everyone in frame is on the index.
- [ ] Minors are not filmed.
- [ ] Outsiders who operate the set (Site Advisor sessions, unseen-space take) sign too.
- [ ] `docs/sets.md` §5 (preflight) lists consent as a filming gate.
