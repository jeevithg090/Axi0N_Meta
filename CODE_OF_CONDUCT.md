# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, welcome people of every background and experience level,
give and take feedback on the work rather than the person, and do not harass anyone.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, tell the team lead, [@jeevithg090](https://github.com/jeevithg090),
privately on GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainers decide what happens next, as
described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
